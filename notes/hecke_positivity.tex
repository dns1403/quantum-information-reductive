\begin{abstract}
We audit an explicit obstruction to positivity of Coxeter-length damping
on the normalized Hecke algebra of $S_3$. We then characterize its full
four-rate cone of symmetric diagonal quantum Markov generators by small
linear matrix inequalities. Under exchange of the two generators the cone
is polyhedral, and the minimum uniform depolarizing correction to length
has a closed formula. Proofs and exact computational certificates are
distinguished throughout; publication-level novelty is unconfirmed.
\end{abstract}
