E005: a global obstruction for the diagonal expectation construction
Date: 2026-09-30. Status: proved below; finite exact checks are supporting
verification, not a replacement for the proof. Novelty is not asserted.

Scope and notation
------------------
Sign convention: A below denotes the positive rate operator; the evolution
generator is L=-A and the semigroup is exp(-tA)=exp(tL).
Let (W,S) be a Coxeter system with finite S and constant parameter q>0,
q!=1. Write p=sqrt(q)-1/sqrt(q), so p!=0. Let M=N_q(W) be the reduced
Hecke von Neumann algebra in its faithful left regular representation,
with canonical faithful normal tracial state tau and orthonormal basis
(T_w) of L2(M,tau). Thus T_s*=T_s, T_s^2=1+p T_s, and
T_s T_w=T_sw+p 1_{ell(sw)<ell(w)} T_w. In particular T_s^{-1}=T_s-p1.
A map is diagonal if Phi(T_w)=m(w)T_w. Markov means normal, unital,
completely positive, and tau-preserving. A rate a:W->[0,infinity) is
proper if every sublevel {w:a(w)<=R}, R<infinity, is finite.

Derivation before implementation
--------------------------------
LEMMA (descent extraction). The coefficient of T_s in T_w T_w* is

  tau(T_s T_w T_w*) = tau(T_w* T_s T_w)
                    = p 1_{ell(sw)<ell(w)}.

The second equality follows from the tracial property and the last from
the Hecke multiplication rule plus orthonormality. This exact coefficient
is nonzero for a left descent even when q<1; no positivity of structure
constants, square root choice beyond sqrt(q)>0, or finite truncation is
being assumed.

LEMMA (fixed basis elements). If Phi is diagonal Markov, J={s:m(s)=1},
then m(w)=1 if and only if w belongs to the standard parabolic W_J.

Proof. If Phi(x)=x, Schwarz gives Phi(x*x)-x*x>=0 and tau of this
difference is zero. Faithfulness of tau gives equality. Applying the same
argument to xx* puts x in the multiplicative domain of Phi. In particular,
if T_w is fixed, T_w T_w* is fixed. Choose a reduced expression w=sv.
Comparing its T_s coefficient under Phi and using descent extraction
gives p m(s)=p, hence m(s)=1. Both T_s^{-1}=T_s-p1 and T_w are fixed,
and T_s^{-1} is in the multiplicative domain. Thus
Phi(T_v)=Phi(T_s^{-1}T_w)=T_s^{-1}T_w=T_v. Induction fixes every letter
of this reduced expression, so w belongs to W_J. Conversely, each T_s
with s in J is fixed and in the multiplicative domain, so all reduced
products with letters in J are fixed. This proves the assertion.

THEOREM (classification of diagonal Markov projections). Every diagonal
Markov idempotent E on M is the canonical tau-preserving conditional
expectation E_J onto N_q(W_J), for a unique subset J of S.

Proof. Idempotence implies m(w) in {0,1}. The fixed-basis lemma implies
m(w)=1_{W_J}(w). The standard-parabolic expectation has exactly these
basis coefficients. Both maps are normal and agree on the weak-* dense
Hecke *-algebra, hence agree globally. Existence of E_J is the usual
trace-preserving expectation in a finite tracial von Neumann algebra;
orthogonal projection in L2 proves its specified coefficients.

COROLLARY (fixed algebra). For any symmetric diagonal Markov semigroup
Phi_t(T_w)=exp(-t a(w))T_w with finite nonnegative rates, its fixed algebra
is N_q(W_J), where J={s:a(s)=0}. Indeed, at every t>0, the preceding
fixed-basis lemma determines the Fourier support of fixed elements.
Normality and the canonical E_J identify the full fixed algebra. In
particular ergodicity is equivalent to a(s)>0 for every s in S.

THEOREM (all positive diagonal expectation sums are bounded). Consider
any sum or positive measurable integral

  A = integral (id-E_x) dmu(x),
  a(w) = integral (1-m_x(w)) dmu(x),

where E_x are diagonal Markov idempotents, and suppose a(s)<infinity for
each s in S. Classify E_x=E_{J_x}. Then

  a(w) <= sum_{s in supp(w)} a(s) <= sum_{s in S} a(s) < infinity.

Proof. The indicator 1_{w notin W_J} equals the indicator that at least
one simple generator in supp(w) is absent from J. Apply the union bound
to these indicators and integrate. Also every proper J omits some s,
so mu({x:J_x!=S}) <= sum_s a(s). The identity projection E_S contributes
zero. Combining equal subsets gives a finite expression

  A=sum_{J proper subset S} c_J(id-E_J),
  c_J=mu({x:J_x=J})>=0, sum_J c_J<infinity.

This is a bounded normal generator. Each factor
exp[-t c_J(id-E_J)]=exp(-tc_J)id+(1-exp(-tc_J))E_J is normal UCP,
tau-preserving, and symmetric. These factors commute because
E_J E_K=E_{J intersect K}. Their finite product establishes the global
normal QMS without extrapolating from any finite matrix calculation.
Every such a on an infinite W is nonproper. Its L2 semigroup is not
compact at t>0, since the diagonal eigenvalues are bounded below by
exp[-t sum_s a(s)]>0 along an infinite orthonormal family.

This bound passes to pointwise limits of these rates whenever each a(s)
has a finite limit. It also rules out unbounded finite-rate constructions
disguised as countably many repeated diagonal expectations: there are
only 2^|S| distinct expectations, and finiteness at the simple generators
forces finite mass on every nonidentity expectation.

AFFINE A2 specialization. Here S={s0,s1,s2}; every proper standard
parabolic has longest word of length at most 3. Therefore every w with
ell(w)>=4 has full support, and any rate in the preceding cone satisfies

  a(w)=C:=sum_{J proper subset S} c_J  for every ell(w)>=4.

Thus there are just eight possible support values (including a(e)=0),
and all infinitely many long words have precisely the same rate. This
is stronger than merely observing that one chosen support-count rate
is bounded.

Limits and unresolved question
------------------------------
The argument excludes only positive sums/integrals of DIAGONAL Markov
projections, plus their pointwise finite-rate limits. It does not show
that every diagonal QMS generator lies in that cone. It does not classify
nondiagonal expectations or forbid cancellations between such maps.
It does not settle whether ell(w)+c 1_{w!=e} admits a global affine A2
QMS for any finite c, or whether another proper diagonal rate exists.
The exact finite A2 conditional-Choi constraints remain necessary local
tests, not sufficient global criteria. These are explicitly unresolved.

The restriction q!=1 is essential: at q=1, for every subgroup H<=W,
the Fourier projection onto VN(H) is diagonal Markov. H need not be a
standard parabolic. In S3, H={e,st,ts} already contradicts the asserted
classification at q=1. Its deformation at p!=0 is not basis-closed:
T_st T_ts = 1+p T_s+p T_sts.

-------------------------------------------------
Searched primary-source leads on 2026-09-30 for "Hecke von Neumann
algebra diagonal conditional expectation parabolic subalgebra multipliers
support Coxeter q" and "Hecke von Neumann word length multiplier
depolarizing semigroup affine positivity". The project's existing

classification statement was located in this bounded search. The proof
above is elementary from the Hecke relations and multiplicative domain,
so should not be promoted as publication-level novelty without further
checking. Relevant source: Martijn Caspers, Mario Klisse, Adam Skalski,

arbitrary von Neumann algebras, Advances in Mathematics 421 (2023),

Completed verification
----------------------
Command: uv run python scripts/verify_affine_expectations.py
Outcome: PASS, exact fractions, no numerical tolerance.
Artifact: scripts/results/affine_expectations_exact.json.

The script uses exact affine permutations to calculate Hecke products
in the full affine A2 algebra. Finite lists specify test inputs only:
intermediate products are never dropped or treated as a truncated
algebra. For 109 words of input length<=8, at q=4, q=1/4, and q=1,
the script performed 2,943 quadratic/inverse/braid action checks and
981 descent-coefficient checks. Product terms reached length 15, which
explicitly verifies that the input length cutoff is not a truncation of
multiplication. It checked full support for every tested word of length
at least 4 and all 64 support-set indicator union-bound cases.

In the genuine six-dimensional finite A2 parabolic, it exhausted all 32
unital basis subsets, testing involution and exact multiplicative closure:
there are exactly the four standard-parabolic subalgebras at q=4 and
q=1/4, but six subgroup algebras at q=1. This verifies the claimed
exception and catches failure to account for the deformation term.
General classifications and nonproperness rest on the proofs above,
not on extending these finite checks by numerical extrapolation.
